Privacy Policy

Marble Pop Factory by NG Studio

Last updated: November 23, 2026

The short version: Marble Pop Factory is a single-player game. We never ask who you are, and your progress is saved on your device. To keep the Game running smoothly we use Google Firebase to collect anonymous gameplay statistics and crash reports, and to send optional notifications. We do not show ads, we do not use your advertising ID, and we never sell your data. You can switch data sharing off in the Game's settings, and notifications off in the Game's or your device's settings, at any time.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how NG Studio, operated by NGENDEV TECHNOLABS LLP ("we", "us", "our"), handles information in the mobile game Marble Pop Factory (the "Game"). The Game has no sign-in, no chat, no leaderboards and no advertising. It uses a few Google services, described below, to measure how the Game is played, to find and fix crashes, to send reminders and news, and to deliver updates.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect information that identifies you. The Game never asks for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, photos, contacts or files, and it does not collect your device's advertising ID. The player name and avatar you choose in the Game stay on your device.

a) Game data saved on your device. To make the Game work, it saves on your device:

  • your level progress, stars and best results;
  • your virtual coin balance and in-game items;
  • your settings, such as sound, music, vibration and notification choices.

This data stays on your device. We cannot see it.

b) Anonymous gameplay statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase). When "Share Usage Data" is on, the Game sends events about how it is played, for example: levels started, won or lost, stars earned, boosters used, coins earned or spent in the Game, screens opened, and whether a notification was opened. These come with technical details: a random app-instance ID created when the Game is installed, device model, operating system and app version, language, and an approximate country or region worked out from your IP address (the IP address itself is not stored by Analytics). This data is not linked to your name or to any account.

c) Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics). When "Share Usage Data" is on and the Game crashes or hits a serious error, it sends a crash report: the technical error details, device model, operating system and app version, device state such as free memory and storage, a random installation ID, and game context such as the current level and screen.

d) Notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging). When "News & Events" is on, Firebase creates a random token so we can deliver push notifications to your device. It is used only to deliver those messages.

3. How we use information

  • to understand which levels are too hard or too easy and keep the Game balanced and fun;
  • to find and fix crashes and bugs;
  • to send the daily reminders and the news you have chosen to receive;
  • to offer Game updates from Google Play.

We do not use any of this information for advertising, we do not build profiles of players, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

4. How data is stored and kept

Your game progress is stored in the Game's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. If you use your device's system backup (for example, Android backup to your Google account), Android may include it in your personal backup. That backup is managed by your device or operating-system provider, and we do not have access to it.

Statistics, crash reports and notification tokens are processed by Google on our behalf, on servers that may be located outside your country. Google protects this data under its own security and privacy commitments. As a guide:

  • Analytics: event data linked to the app-instance ID is kept for 2 months; after that only aggregated reports remain;
  • Crashlytics: crash reports are kept for 90 days;
  • Cloud Messaging: the notification token is kept until you switch "News & Events" off or uninstall the Game, after which Google removes it according to its own schedule.

5. Third-party services

The Game uses these services, all provided by Google:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase, for anonymous gameplay statistics, with advertising-ID collection and ad personalization switched off;
  • Firebase Crashlytics, for crash reports;
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging, for push notifications;
  • Google Play In-App Updates, which lets Google Play tell you about a new version and install it. This is handled by Google Play, and the Game sends no data of its own for it.

You can read how Google handles this data in the Google Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and on the Firebase privacy page (firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

The Game does not include any advertising, social-media or payment services. The app store you downloaded it from (for example, Google Play) may also collect information under its own privacy policy.

6. Notifications

With your permission, the Game can show two kinds of notifications:

  • Daily reminders at about 9 AM and 7 PM (your device's time), about things like your next level, your free spin or the daily challenge. These are created on your device from your local game data. Nothing is sent to us to make them.
  • News & events, sent by us through Firebase Cloud Messaging, about new levels, events and rewards.

To stop daily reminders, turn off the Game's Daily reminders notifications in your device's settings (Settings → Apps → Marble Pop Factory → Notifications). To stop news, switch off News & Events in the Game's Settings → Notifications. You can also turn off all notifications for the Game in your device's settings.

7. Your choices

  • Share Usage Data: switch it off in Settings → Privacy & Legal and the Game stops sending gameplay statistics and crash reports.
  • Notifications: switch news on or off in the Game's Settings → Notifications; turn daily reminders (or all notifications) off in your device's notification settings for the Game.
  • Updates: you can decline or postpone an optional update. Some important updates may be required to keep playing (see our Terms of Service).

8. Children's privacy

Marble Pop Factory is designed to be friendly for players of all ages, including children. The Game does not ask for or knowingly collect personal information such as names, email addresses or precise location from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

The anonymous statistics, crash reports and notification tokens described above use random identifiers. They are used only to run, maintain and improve the Game, never for advertising, behavioral profiling or contacting a child. The Game does not collect the advertising ID, and ad personalization is switched off.

Parents and guardians can turn off data sharing and news at any time in the Game's settings, and all notifications in the device's settings. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example, by emailing us), please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Permissions

The Game uses these Android permissions:

  • Vibration (VIBRATE), for haptic feedback when marbles pop and land in cups. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, Android 13 and later), to show daily reminders and news. Android asks for your permission first.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED), only to set the daily reminders again after your device restarts.
  • Internet and network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE), to send statistics and crash reports, receive notifications and check for updates. The Game itself is fully playable offline.
  • Receive push messages and keep the device awake briefly (c2dm.permission.RECEIVE, WAKE_LOCK), used by Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver notifications.
  • Install referrer (BIND_GET_INSTALL_REFERRER_SERVICE), used by Analytics to learn which Google Play page or campaign led to the install.

The Game does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

10. Data deletion

You are always in control:

  • Reset progress: use the reset option in the Game's settings to erase your saved progress and coins. (Your notification and data-sharing choices are kept.)
  • Stop sharing: switch off "Share Usage Data". Previously sent data is deleted automatically after the periods in section 4.
  • Clear data or uninstall: clearing the Game's data (device Settings → Apps → Marble Pop Factory → Storage → Clear data) or uninstalling it removes everything on your device and resets the random IDs, so new data can no longer be linked to old data.
  • Ask us: you can contact us to request deletion of data held by our service providers. Because we do not know who you are, we may need your help (for example, the date and device you played on) to find it.

Deleted progress cannot be recovered.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we add new features. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above, and for important changes we may also show a notice in the Game.
