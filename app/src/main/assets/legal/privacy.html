Privacy Policy

Marble Pop Factory by Puzzel Lab

Last updated: November 5, 2026

The short version: Marble Pop Factory is an offline, single-player game. We do not ask who you are, we do not collect personal information, and nothing about you is sent to us or to anyone else.

No account No ads No tracking No real-money purchases Saved on your device only

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Puzzel Lab ("we", "us", "our") handles information in the mobile game Marble Pop Factory (the "Game"). The Game works completely offline. It has no sign-in, no chat, no leaderboards, no advertising, and no analytics.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The Game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, photos, contacts, or any other information that identifies you, and it does not use advertising IDs or device identifiers.

To make the Game work, it saves a small amount of game data on your device, such as:

  • your level progress, stars, and best results;
  • your virtual coin balance and in-game items;
  • your settings, like sound, music, and vibration on/off.

This data is never sent to us, and we cannot see it.

3. How data is stored

Your game data is stored locally in the Game's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. The Game does not upload it to any server.

If you have turned on your device's system backup (for example, Android backup to your Google account), Android may include the Game's local data in your personal device backup so your progress can be restored on a new device. That backup is managed by your device or operating-system provider under their own privacy policy; we do not receive or have access to it.

4. Third-party services

None. The Game does not include any third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, social-media, or payment SDKs, and it does not share or sell any data to third parties.

The app store you downloaded the Game from (for example, Google Play) may collect information about your download and device under its own privacy policy. Please review that store's policy for details.

5. Children's privacy

Marble Pop Factory is designed to be friendly for players of all ages, including children. Because the Game does not collect, use, or disclose personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), consistent with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws such as the GDPR.

Parents and guardians: if you believe a child has somehow sent us personal information (for example, by emailing us), please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

6. Permissions

The Game requests only one Android permission:

  • Vibration (android.permission.VIBRATE), used for haptic feedback when marbles pop and land in cups. You can turn vibration off at any time in the Game's settings.

The Game does not request access to the internet, your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or files.

7. Data deletion

You are always in control of your game data:

  • Reset progress: use the reset option in the Game's settings to erase your saved progress and coins.
  • Clear data: go to your device's Settings → Apps → Marble Pop Factory → Storage → Clear data.
  • Uninstall: uninstalling the Game removes all of its data from your device.

Since we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Deleted progress cannot be recovered.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if we add new features. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above. If a future version of the Game ever needs to collect data, we will update this policy and, where required by law, ask for consent first (including verifiable parental consent for children).
