🔮Introduction

Welcome to Marble Pop – Color Sort Puzzle ("Marble Pop" or the "Game"), a free single-player puzzle game developed and published by NGENDEV TECHNOLABS LLP (NG Studio), Rajkot, Gujarat, India ("we", "us" or "our"). In the Game you pop jelly blocks and sort colorful marbles into matching cups. This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you play, how it is used, and the choices you have.

The short version: there is no account and no sign-up, we never ask for your real name, email or phone number, and your progress is saved only on your device. The Game has no ads, does not use your advertising ID, sells nothing for real money and has no real-money gambling. To find bugs and improve the Game, it sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase, and it can show you notifications; you can turn both off at any time.

By installing or playing Marble Pop, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

📦1. Information we collect

Information you give us

Only a player name and avatar of your choice for your in-game profile, which can be any nickname. They are stored on your device and are never sent to us or shown to anyone else: the Game has no multiplayer, chat or leaderboards. We never ask for your real name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts, location or any other personal information inside the Game.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the Game saves a small amount of data locally on your device only:

  • Your player name, avatar, current level, the stars you earned on each level and your coin balance
  • Your boosters (Hammer, Magnet, Extra Slots and Shuffle), the marble skins you own and use, and the worlds you have unlocked or chosen
  • Your statistics and achievements, treasure chests opened, levels unlocked early, your daily login streak, Lucky Spin, Daily Challenge and free gift timers
  • Your settings, such as music, sound effects, vibration, News & Events and Share Usage Data
  • Which special blocks and worlds you have already been introduced to, so tutorials are not shown twice

This data is not sent to us, except for the anonymous usage statistics described in Analytics, crash data and notifications. It is not linked to your identity.

Information collected by our partners

Google (through Google Play and Google Firebase) may automatically collect certain technical information, as described in Google Play and Android and Analytics, crash data and notifications below.

📱2. Device permissions

Marble Pop asks Android for these permissions:

  • Vibration, for the small buzz when blocks pop and marbles land in cups. You can turn it off with the Vibration switch in Settings.
  • Internet access and network state, to send anonymous usage statistics and crash reports (if you allow it), to receive notifications, and to check for Game updates. The whole Game can be played offline.
  • Notifications, for the daily reminders and for news about new levels and events. On Android 13 and later the Game asks you first, and you can say no.
  • Run at startup, used only to set the daily reminders again after your device restarts.
  • Receive push messages and keep the device awake briefly, used by Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver notifications.
  • Install referrer, used by Google Analytics for Firebase to learn which Google Play page led to the install.

The Game does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, files, phone calls or messages, and it does not collect your device's advertising ID.

🚫3. No ads, no purchases

Marble Pop does not show advertising and does not include any advertising software. The advertising ID permission is removed from the Game, and ad personalization is switched off in Google Analytics, so no data is collected for ads.

Nothing in the Game is sold for real money. Coins, boosters, marble skins, worlds, chests and other rewards are earned only by playing. The Lucky Spin uses free virtual coins only; coins cannot be bought, sold or cashed out. There is no real-money gambling of any kind.

🔄4. Google Play and Android

Updates

The Game uses Google Play's in-app update service to check whether a newer version is available and, if you agree, to download and install it without leaving the Game. This is carried out by the Google Play Store app on your device under Google's own terms and privacy policy. Apart from the anonymous statistics described below (for example, that an update was offered or installed), we do not receive any information about you from it.

Device backup

If backup is turned on in your Android settings, Android may include the Game's saved progress in your device backup to your Google account, so it can be restored on a new phone. This backup is handled entirely by Google; we cannot access it. You can turn backup off in your device settings.

Google Play

When you download the Game, Google Play processes information such as your Google account and device details under Google's Privacy Policy. We may receive anonymous, aggregated statistics from Google Play (for example, the number of installs per country or crash counts) that do not identify you.

📊5. Analytics, crash data and notifications

Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand which levels are too hard or too easy and what to improve, the Game uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It records anonymous events, such as screens opened, the tutorial, levels started, won or lost (with stars, combos and boosters used), retries and continues, coins earned or spent in the Game, rewards claimed, settings changed, your answer to the notification prompt, notifications shown or opened, and update prompts, plus your highest level. Each event comes with technical information: an app-instance ID (a random number created for this installation of the Game, not your name or advertising ID), device model, Android version, the Game's language and version, approximate location (country or region, worked out from your IP address) and the time of the event.

We do not use these statistics for advertising, and we never link them to your name, email or any account.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the Game crashes or hits a serious error, Firebase Crashlytics sends us a crash report so we can fix the problem. It contains technical details: what the Game was doing when it crashed (for example, the screen that was open and the current level), the device model, Android version, memory and storage state, the Game version and an installation ID (a random number for this installation of the Game).

Turning statistics and crash reports off

Both are on by default and can be turned off at any time with the Share Usage Data switch in the Game's Settings (Privacy & Legal). While it is off, no usage statistics or crash reports are sent.

Google processes this information for us as a service provider under the Firebase terms and Google's Privacy Policy, and keeps it only for a limited time (at most 14 months for usage statistics and 90 days for crash reports). Learn more at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Daily reminders (on your device)

If you allow notifications, the Game shows up to two daily reminders, at about 9 AM and 7 PM your device's time, about things like your next level, your free Lucky Spin, your login reward or the Daily Challenge. They are created on your device from your local game data, and nothing is sent to us to create them. To turn them off, switch off the Game's Daily reminders notifications in your Android settings.

News and events (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

We may also send news about new levels, events and rewards through Firebase Cloud Messaging, which gives this installation of the Game a random token so that messages can reach it. Messages go to everyone who uses the Game, never to a named person, and we do not link the token to you. Turn them off with the News & Events switch in the Game's Settings (which also deletes the token) or in your Android settings.

Your device or Google Play may separately offer to share crash reports with developers. That sharing is controlled by your device settings and Google's own privacy policy.

⚙️6. How we use information

The limited information involved in Marble Pop is used only to:

  • Save your progress, coins, unlocked items and settings so you can continue where you left off
  • Find and fix crashes and bugs, and see which levels and features need balancing or improving (anonymous statistics and crash reports, if you allow them)
  • Remind you about your next level and daily rewards, and tell you about new levels and events (if you allow notifications)
  • Offer you updates to the Game
  • Reply to you if you contact us for support

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it to build profiles about you.

🤝7. Data sharing

Your progress stays on your device. Information is shared only in these cases:

  • Google Firebase: the anonymous statistics, crash reports and notification token described in Analytics, crash data and notifications are processed by Google on our behalf.
  • Legal reasons: if we are required to do so by law, or to protect the rights, safety or property of our users, ourselves or others.
  • Business transfers: if the Game is sold or transferred to another developer, this policy (or one at least as protective) will continue to apply.

🗑️8. Data retention and deletion

Your game data stays on your device until you remove it. You can delete it at any time by:

  • Using Settings → Reset Progress inside the Game, which erases your levels, stars, coins, items, statistics and achievements (your notification and data-sharing choices are kept), or
  • Uninstalling Marble Pop, or clearing the app's storage in your device settings, which also resets the random IDs so new data can no longer be linked to old data

Anonymous statistics and crash reports are kept by Google Firebase only for the limited time described in section 5. Data collected by Google Play is kept according to Google's own retention policies. If you email us, we keep your message only as long as needed to help you, and then delete it.

🧸9. Children's privacy

Marble Pop is a cheerful puzzle game that is friendly for players of all ages. It does not ask anyone, of any age, for personal information such as names, email addresses or precise location, it has no chat, no ads and no real-money purchases, and it does not collect the advertising ID.

The anonymous statistics, crash reports and notification token described above use random identifiers and are used only to run, maintain and improve the Game, never for advertising, behavioral profiling or contacting a child. Parents and guardians can turn off Share Usage Data and News & Events at any time in the Game's Settings, and all notifications in the device's settings.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under the minimum age set by the law of their country. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to us (for example, by emailing us), please contact us at store.ngdtechnolab@gmail.com and we will delete it.

🛡️10. Security

Game data is stored in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot normally access. We work only with reputable partners that use industry-standard safeguards.

No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but because we hold so little information, there is very little that could ever be exposed.

🎚️11. Your choices

  • Stop sharing statistics and crash reports: turn off Share Usage Data in the Game's Settings.
  • Turn off news: use the News & Events switch in the Game's Settings.
  • Turn off daily reminders or all notifications: use your Android notification settings for the Game.
  • Change your nickname: edit your player name and avatar in your profile at any time.
  • Turn off vibration: use the Vibration switch in the Game's Settings.
  • Updates: you can decline or postpone an optional update.
  • Delete your data: use Reset Progress in Settings, or uninstall the Game.

🌍12. Your rights around the world

You can play Marble Pop from anywhere. Information collected by Google Firebase may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, under appropriate safeguards.

India

We follow the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under it, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. You have the right to ask what personal data about you we hold, to have it corrected or erased, and to raise a grievance with us using the contact details below. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR)

Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports are processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the Game stable and improving it; you can object at any time by turning off Share Usage Data. Notifications are sent only if you allow them. You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

California (CCPA / CPRA)

California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected and to request its deletion. We do not sell or share personal information, and the Game shows no ads. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Since we do not hold your game data on our servers and the statistics we receive are not linked to your identity, most requests are best handled by the in-game controls above. You are always welcome to contact us for help; because we do not know who you are, we may need details such as the date and device you played on to find data held by our service providers.
